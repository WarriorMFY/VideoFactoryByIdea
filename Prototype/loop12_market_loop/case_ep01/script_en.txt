Five small edits that make any video look more expensive.
Number one: cut on the beat, not on the silence.
Number two: keep your subtitles inside the safe area, at least forty pixels from the bottom edge.
Number three: normalize the audio to minus sixteen LUFS, and never let the dialogue peak above minus one dB.
Number four: export at fifteen megabits per second for a clean upload, and always check the first three seconds.
Number five: before you publish, watch your own video once with the sound off.
That is the whole checklist. Save it, and your next edit will take half the time.
